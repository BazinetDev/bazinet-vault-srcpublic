The reason they gave

What they would be able to see

  • Sign-ins, sign-outs and errors your account has recorded
  • Devices and sessions, including ones that have ended
  • When items were created or changed — never what is in them
  • Technical checks on whether your account is in a healthy state
  • Diagnostic logs your devices record while syncing — which steps ran and what failed. Never your agenda, your messages, or any password.

What they still cannot see

  • Any password, note or file inside your vault
  • Your master password, or anything derived from it
  • Approving this cannot change that — the key was never ours to use

Every time support opens your log it is recorded in your own account history, so you can check exactly when it was read.
