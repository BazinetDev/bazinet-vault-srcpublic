Why use a generated password?

Reused or simple passwords are the No. 1 cause of account takeovers. A long, random password — different for every site — is the single best thing you can do for your online security. This tool builds one using your browser's cryptographically secure random generator, so each character is truly unpredictable and never leaves your device.

Generate one here, then store it (and the rest of your logins) in your free Bazinet Vault, where everything is encrypted on your device with Argon2id + AES-256 before it's ever saved.
