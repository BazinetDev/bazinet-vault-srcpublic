Zero-knowledge by design

Your secrets never leave your device unencrypted

When you sign in, your master password is stretched with Argon2id into a key that never leaves your browser. That key wraps a random vault key, and that vault key encrypts every entry. The server only ever receives sealed, unreadable blobs.

  • Master password is never transmitted or stored
  • Server stores ciphertext only — a breach reveals nothing usable
  • Changing your password re-wraps the vault, never re-exposes it

Questions

Frequently asked

What does "zero-knowledge" actually mean?

It means the encryption and decryption of your vault happen on your device, not on our servers. We receive and store only encrypted blobs. Because your master password and keys never leave your browser, we have no technical way to read your data — even if we wanted to, or were compelled to.

What happens if I forget my master password?

Your data is encrypted with a key derived from that password, and there is no recovery backdoor by design — we cannot reset it for you, and support cannot be talked into it. There is one way back in, and only you can set it up: a recovery key. It is 32 random characters generated in your browser and shown once, and it opens the same vault your master password does. Create one from the vault menu and keep it somewhere physically safe. Without a recovery key, forgetting your master password means the vault cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us.

Is Bazinet Vault really free?

Yes. It runs on Cloudflare's free tier and is supported by unobtrusive ads on these public pages (never on your vault). No credit card, no trial, no upsell.

Should I store my most critical secrets here?

For everyday logins, yes — your vault is end-to-end encrypted and only you hold the key. Two honest caveats, though: Bazinet Vault is an independent project that hasn't yet had a formal third-party security audit, and there's no password-recovery backdoor — if you forget your master password and haven't set up a recovery key, the data can't be recovered by anyone. So set up a recovery key, and for irreplaceable, high-stakes secrets also keep a separate backup and don't rely on any single tool. See our Terms for the full details.

How is the breach check private?

We use k-anonymity: your browser hashes the password with SHA-1 and sends only the first five characters of that hash to look up matches. The full password and full hash never leave your device.
