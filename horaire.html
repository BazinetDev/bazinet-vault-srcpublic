Connect Omnivox

Your homework, added by itself

How is this different from the browser extension?

Two ways to read the same school account. You only need one, and Bazinet picks for you.

The extension reads LÉA and Mio inside your own browser, on the Omnivox session you are already signed in to. Your Omnivox password is never involved and nothing about your school account leaves this computer. It only works in a desktop browser with the extension installed.

Connecting Omnivox does the sign-in on our server instead, which is the only way this can work on iPhone and Android, where a browser extension cannot exist. Your password is kept encrypted and is decrypted for one sign-in at a time.

When both are available the extension is used first, because it keeps your password out of it entirely. Connecting Omnivox changes nothing on a computer that already has the extension — it is there for your phone.

What happens to your password

It is encrypted on this device with your Bazinet account key, the same way your agenda is. Our server stores it only as ciphertext it cannot read.

To fetch your homework, your password is decrypted here and sent to our server for that one sign-in. The server uses it and keeps nothing.

Omnivox may occasionally ask for a CAPTCHA when a sign-in comes from a server. If that happens, sync stops and tells you — sign in on Omnivox once and it resumes.

Omnivox will email you a code the first time, because the sync runs from our server and not your phone. We keep the “remember this device” cookie it gives us afterwards, encrypted, so it only asks once. Disconnecting deletes it.

The email Omnivox sends about the new sign-in may say it came from an iPhone in the United States. That's our server, not someone else.
