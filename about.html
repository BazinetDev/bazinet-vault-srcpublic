About Bazinet.net

Independent software company · Montréal, Québec

Bazinet.net builds simple, private software for everyday use. All of its products work with a single Bazinet account.

Products

  • Horaire CVM — the student agenda for Cégep du Vieux Montréal: classes, homework, exams and due dates in one place.
  • Bazinet Vault — a password manager with a browser extension and a desktop app.
  • Free security tools — a password generator and a breach checker, with no account needed.
  • Bazinet Aim Trainer — a companion app for Valorant players.

Our principles

  • Privacy. Personal content is encrypted on your device. No advertising, no third-party tracking, and no selling of data.
  • Independence. No outside investors. The products are free to use, with an optional Pro subscription.
