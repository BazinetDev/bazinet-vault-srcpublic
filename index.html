Good to know

Questions

Who makes Bazinet?

It's an independent project — small tools built and maintained by one person, not a company chasing your data. Questions or bugs go straight to support@bazinet.net.

Is Bazinet really free?

Yes. Horaire, the vault, the quick tools and the Aim Trainer are free to start, with no credit card. An optional Pro plan adds extras to Horaire and the vault and helps cover server costs: $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year (7 days free), a $9.99 term pass with no subscription, or Family at $5.99 a month for up to 5 people.

Can you see my schedule?

No. Horaire encrypts your agenda on your device before it is saved to your account, with a key only your password can produce. The schedule PDF you import is read in your browser and never uploaded.

Can you read my passwords or vault data?

No. The vault is zero-knowledge: your data is encrypted in your browser with a key derived from your master password (Argon2id → AES-256-GCM) before anything is sent. We only ever store ciphertext, so we have no technical way to read it — even if compelled to.

Is Horaire made by the Cégep?

No. Horaire CVM is an independent app built for Cégep du Vieux Montréal students. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Cégep, Omnivox or Skytech — always check Omnivox for anything that matters, like an exam date.

Do I need an account to use the tools?

The password generator and the breach check need nothing at all. Horaire, the vault and the Aim Trainer use one free Bazinet account (email, Google or a passkey) that works on the website, the browser extension and the desktop apps.

Will the Aim Trainer get my Valorant account banned?

The core app is read-only — it uses the official Riot API and reads your own running client, exactly like public rank-trackers, and never touches the game process. It ships with one clearly-labelled optional "Snap Tap" toggle that is against Valorant's rules and is off by default; don't enable that one for Valorant. Everything else is safe by design.
