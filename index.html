For anyone with too many passwords

Bazinet Vault keeps passwords only you can read.

Your vault is encrypted in your browser with Argon2id and AES 256, using a key from your master password. The server stores ciphertext and nothing else.

  • Web, Chrome extension and Mac
  • One master password, never sent to the server
  • Lose it without a recovery key and nobody can open the vault, including us
Open the VaultHow it works

What happens to a password you save

1

You type your master password

It stays on your device. Bazinet never receives it, so nobody here can unlock your vault.

2

Argon2id turns it into a key

In your browser, and slow on purpose, so guessing a master password costs real time for each try.

3

AES 256 locks every entry

Only the locked copy is saved. A copy of our database would show nothing but scrambled bytes.
