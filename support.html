How support works

Response times

Live chat: right away when someone is online. Tickets: usually within one business day. Security reports: acknowledged within 3 business days.

What we will never ask for

Your password, your master password, your recovery key, or a code we sent you. Anyone who asks for one is not us.

What support can see

Your messages here and your account details. Never your vault or your agenda: they are encrypted on your device.

Security issues

Email support@bazinet.net with SECURITY in the subject, and please don't post them publicly.

Privacy requests

Ask to see, correct or delete your personal information. We answer within 30 days, as Québec's Law 25 requires.

Languages

English and French, in chat, tickets and email.
