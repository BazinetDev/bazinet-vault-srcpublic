Bazinet Vault

Create your master password

This encrypts your vault on your device. It is separate from your account password and cannot be recovered — there is no reset, no support override, and no copy anywhere else. If you forget it, the vault is gone.

It also has to resist offline guessing: the attack that matters is someone who has stolen a copy of the encrypted database and can guess for as long as they like, without us being able to slow them down. Length and unpredictability are what defeat that. A generated passphrase is the easy way to get both.

Write it down and keep it somewhere safe until you have it memorised. Losing it means losing the vault.
